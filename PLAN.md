# Plan

Races through an open town: on foot, then by car, by boat and by plane. One new thing a
release, each seen on the stand before the phone.

## Done

- 0.4 The open town, grown from a seed a cell at a time; the runner on foot: a finger runs
  her, sideways turns her, a flick up jumps, a flick down slides.
- 0.5 A chase: her double on fire runs off through the streets, and is to be caught.
- 0.6 The town alive: cars about the streets and parked along the curbs, people on the
  pavements; the double burns through, her light and glow on all round her.
- 0.7 The cars run into the double: she is flung, falls, lies and gets up again; a moment to
  catch her.
- 0.8 The first mission: five golems loose about the town, to be found, run down and caught;
  caught, each falls apart for good.
- 0.9 Her hammer: thrown by itself at a golem near enough, it knocks the plate off its brow;
  the plates won fill five slots over the frame. A new icon.
- 0.10 The hammer thrown from nearer, within 10 meters.
- 0.11 The hammer a stonemason's maul.
- 0.12 Run down to the end: within reach, she stops and throws her mallet of planks and iron.
- 0.13 A new look: early-2000s town realism in place of print; worked surfaces, soft light, haze.
- 0.14 The opening: down to the square, the dark, the blow into the glass, the name in gold.
- 0.15 The dispatcher's room: the briefing on the set before a mission, the report after it.
- 0.16 The golem runs on when reached and the mallet catches it up; all a little quicker.
- 0.17 The dispatcher's room is her head: walls of her brick round the street seen through her eyes.
- 0.18 Russian, by the phone's language or the game's own.
- 0.19 A tap jumps, two taps leap, a flick up glides; over cars and onto roofs; autosave.
- 0.20 The folder on the roofs: lifts up, roof to roof, rival golems climbing; the tube's letters.
- 0.21 A tap is the mallet's (strike or throw); a flick jumps, a second flick goes on up; she
  glides by herself from high up; lifts open by themselves; the folder marked green; rivals later.
- 0.22 Indoors: places made from a seed; a house's hall, front door to yard door, with a lift
  that calls, opens, rides and lets her out by itself.
- 0.23 A game that begins with the folder no longer stops at once.

## Next

- More plans indoors, of the same parts, for other missions: a stairwell, offices, a garage,
  a hotel's lobby; a mission that starts or ends indoors.

- The new look carried on: people of natural build with simple faces and big folds in their
  clothes; cars in the manner of the eighties, angular saloons, long coupes and open tops, a
  little chrome; balconies, signs and lights of the evening, pink and blue.

- The race against the clock: gates at crossings strung into a course, a clock, a mark at the
  edge of the frame pointing to the next gate.
- A speed trap: a point on a street where the speed run through it is what counts.
- Races against rivals: runners of their own, kept apart from the people and traffic about.
- More life about the town: people crossing at the crossings, cars that hoot and swerve.
- A car: parked at a curb, run up to and got into; left where it was got out of. The car's own
  feel: mass, grip, how it takes a corner. The finger to lead it as it leads her; whether it
  needs a pedal of its own is to be tried.
- The sea along one side of the town, a promenade, canals; a boat.
- A plane.
- The room in her head to go with her in the car, the boat and the plane: the same room, what
  she sees through the screen changing with what she is in. Its keys to do more: a choice of
  missions, the best times kept.
- A start and a finish to every race; what is won and kept.
- Sound: steps, breath, the fire.
- The project's page, with the game working in the browser.
