# Plan

Races through an open town: on foot, then by car, by boat and by plane. One new thing a
release, each seen on the stand before the phone.

## Next

The missions in order, each teaching a thing; then the town is hers.

1. The hunt: in the streets only (the lifts shut), learning to run them.
2. The folder: over the roofs, learning to jump, climb and glide, a way to be worked out.
3. The delivery: duty pickups, open-topped, stand here and there in the streets. Find one, drive
   it to the factory, take on a load for the store, carry it there, drive home.
4. After that, free: drive about, get out, walk, climb the roofs, find the duty pickups, find
   home. A mission ends at home; at home the missions done can be played again, or the next one
   taken.

The releases toward it, one thing each:

- A stretch of the town fixed for ever, the same in every town grown: an embankment with a few
  known houses, among them home, one large house; then what home is like inside.
- The factory and the store, each a building of its own kind, plain to see; their signs; a
  load on the pickup's bed.
- The delivery as mission three; then home as the end of every mission, and the choice there.
- The other buttons put to work as their things come: the wheel in a car, the jet pack, the map,
  the help, a throw and a lift of a thing; buttons as part of the controls where a gesture is
  not enough.
- A river through the town: a channel of water in place of a street now and then, stone
  embankments and steps down, bridges at the crossings (stone arches, an iron one, a foot
  bridge), the traffic and the chase going over them; boats moored along it.
Asset sets kept apart from these sources (the meshes are not in the repository; see the README)
are waiting for the work below. Each item is one release or a few.

- Still meshes in the renderer: the people's path without bones, for whatever stands and does
  not walk (trees, hedges, furniture, cars), drawn many times over from one copy.
- Greenery: trees along the streets, hedges and low bushes round the squares and along the
  promenade, flower beds, a green maze in a park; summer here, the other seasons for another
  town.
- Cars as meshes instead of boxes, their wheels turning on their own: ordinary saloons,

- A mission in another, colder town: its own townspeople in coats and jumpers.
